Sources: DOSM Population Table (state/district/DUN, 2022 estimates), SPR Daftar Pemilih Tambahan (Aug 2022, GE15), Tindak Malaysia Historical Election Results (2013, 2018, 2023 Kelantan state elections). Two DUNs (Pantai Irama, Apam Putra) were known by different names before the 2018 redelineation (Perupok, Bukit Tuku) — their 2013 result is shown as the closest predecessor seat, not a guaranteed identical boundary.
